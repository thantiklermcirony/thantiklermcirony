## The Bounded Observer

A research programme about what an observer can record, read, retain, combine and change.

**UHL** supplies the operational geometry of positive records and bounded normalized readings under named premises. **SIH** studies regularity and concentration in specified field models. The common library closes the declared finite complex record operations into a loop; its branches identify the measurements, dynamics and tests needed in their subjects.

| Branch | Work |
| --- | --- |
| Physics and SIH | Record geometry, light, composition, fields, matter, gravity and state selection |
| IDA | Measurement and intervention research; IDA Live and StateAtlas |
| Arc Virtual Cell Challenge | Framework-led cellular predictor, official competition results and development |
| Observatory cell-response flights | Earlier frozen trials (Flights 01 and 02) and their failed success gates |
| Living systems | Hormesis, redox, recovery, population and pharmacological models |
| Scientific AI | Evidence, retained warrant, dependency-aware memory and engineering |
| Arithmetic | Exact projection and refinement, with separate approximation questions |
| Evidence and decisions | Domain-specific research and its retained source history |

The non-neural, rule-based **31-EchoFull** competition predictor recorded a validation-panel leaderboard entry rank of **221 of 1,416 entries** and overall challenge score **0.2102**, observed on 10 October 2026. This is a dated validation result, not a final standing. [Method and dated evidence](https://github.com/thantiklermcirony/bounded-observer/tree/main/branches/vcc). Later variants and the earlier laboratory are separately recorded.

The current capstone and supporting library were received by SSRN on 13 October 2026 and await completeness review. <details>
<summary><strong>The earlier six-gate entrance</strong></summary>

An observer with finite access cannot see everything about the world it measures. What must it remember to predict what happens next? Which actions remain useful before a boundary is reached? **The Bounded Observer** follows those questions through six gates: access, state, action, chart and boundary, geometry, and prediction.

The [public site](https://thantiklermcirony.github.io/bounded-observer/) makes the models explorable through twelve interactive demonstrations. The [repository](https://github.com/thantiklermcirony/bounded-observer) holds the assumptions, capstone manuscript, executable checks, IDA Live prototype and claim-by-claim atlas. Each claim carries a status and a next test, so a mathematical consequence, a simulation, an empirical result and an open hypothesis remain distinct.

The central composition result is conditional: continuous, associative, strictly monotone composition with a neutral state admits an additive coordinate with a bound at an unreachable horizon. Boundedness by itself does **not** establish that law for every system, a particular geometry, or a general biological prediction.

<summary><strong>Earlier programme, research records and contributions</strong></summary>

The earlier work remains available as a source record. The [Boundedness Atlas](https://boundedness-atlas.madmanmuzza.chatgpt.site/) contains a [manuscript library](https://boundedness-atlas.madmanmuzza.chatgpt.site/papers/), [eight connected contributions](https://boundedness-atlas.madmanmuzza.chatgpt.site/discoveries/), [predictions and failure criteria](https://boundedness-atlas.madmanmuzza.chatgpt.site/predictions/), and a [biology exhibit](https://boundedness-atlas.madmanmuzza.chatgpt.site/biology/). Its 41 manuscript records include historical versions; they are not 41 independent discoveries. The [programme direction](https://github.com/thantiklermcirony/boundedness-atlas/blob/main/PROGRAMME_DIRECTION.md) and [observer lens](https://github.com/thantiklermcirony/boundedness-atlas/blob/main/OBSERVER_LENS.md) preserve the wider synthesis.

- [Empirical Architecture](https://github.com/thantiklermcirony/empirical-architecture) preserves manuscripts, mathematical foundations, a [theory atlas, proofs and executable certificates](https://github.com/thantiklermcirony/empirical-architecture/tree/main/research/theory-atlas-v0.1), counterexamples and corrections.
- [Empirical Observatory](https://github.com/thantiklermcirony/empirical-observatory) preserves laboratory software and research records. The hosted interactive Observatory has restricted access; public source and a working hosted exhibit have separate release states.
- [Recovery Lab](https://github.com/thantiklermcirony/empirical-observatory/tree/main/research/recovery-lab) compared 214 older female mice; follow-up coverage and the added-history improvement missed the declared gates.
- [Virtual Cell Flight 01](https://github.com/thantiklermcirony/empirical-observatory/tree/main/research/virtual-cell) and [Flight 02](https://github.com/thantiklermcirony/empirical-observatory/tree/main/research/virtual-cell-flight02) retain their failed held-out success gates.
- [Cancer Lab](https://github.com/thantiklermcirony/empirical-observatory/blob/main/CANCER_LAB.md) contains an experimental recovery model and proposed tests. It does not establish a cancer treatment or clinical efficacy.
- [Active Context](https://github.com/thantiklermcirony/empirical-observatory/tree/main/automation/active-context) is a runnable diagnostic. Its first comparison did not establish superiority over conventional dependency checking.
- [IDA / StateAtlas](https://github.com/thantiklermcirony/ida-stateatlas) preserves measurement and intervention prototypes. Hardware validity and useful added physiological prediction remain open.

Published model-specific results: [Hormesis, *Dose-Response*](https://doi.org/10.1177/15593258261469171) and [Glutathione homeostasis, *Redox Biochemistry and Chemistry*](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.rbc.2026.100084). The redox paper distinguishes kinetic calibration from a prospective cancer prediction. The hormesis paper's unrestricted necessity, universal-peak and finite-amplitude claims are withdrawn in the author's [mathematical amendment](https://github.com/thantiklermcirony/bounded-observer/blob/main/papers/library-2026-10-12/pdf/hormesis_amendment.pdf) and [proposed correction](https://github.com/thantiklermcirony/bounded-observer/blob/main/papers/library-2026-10-12/pdf/hormesis_corrigendum.pdf); the proposal is not an issued journal corrigendum.

Engineering contribution records: [Ray Serve](https://github.com/ray-project/ray/pull/66039), [NeuroGym](https://github.com/neurogym/neurogym/pull/295), [Pertpy](https://github.com/scverse/pertpy/pull/1098), [Graphiti history](https://github.com/getzep/graphiti/pull/1867) and [Graphiti timestamps](https://github.com/getzep/graphiti/pull/1866). Each pull request has its own review status; submission is not acceptance or validation of the wider framework.

Published results, conditional proofs, passing software checks, failed empirical gates and prospective hypotheses should be read at their own evidence levels.
