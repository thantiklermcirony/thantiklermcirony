## What does a measurement leave out?

Two systems can give the same reading and respond differently to the next challenge. The programme studies which histories, resources and mechanisms must be retained to predict that difference—and when learning it can still change a useful action.

Its wider ambition is a connected scientific account of systems and observers acting within finite capacities. Mathematical models supply conditional consequences; measurements test their applicability; failed predictions identify what must change. UHL, predictive closure, temporal dynamics, hormesis, redox, IDA and scientific AI contribute different parts of that account.

**Start with the public Boundedness Atlas above.** The laboratory software, research records and downloads are also [public on GitHub](https://github.com/thantiklermcirony/empirical-observatory). The hosted interactive Observatory currently has restricted access. A source package and a working hosted exhibit have separate release states.

## How the programme fits together

**Question → model and assumptions → prediction → observation or intervention → result → revised understanding.**

- **Public exhibits:** [Boundedness Atlas](https://boundedness-atlas.madmanmuzza.chatgpt.site/).
- **Papers and foundations:** [Empirical Architecture](https://github.com/thantiklermcirony/empirical-architecture).
- **Laboratory software and evidence:** [Empirical Observatory](https://github.com/thantiklermcirony/empirical-observatory).
- **Sensing and action:** [IDA / StateAtlas](https://github.com/thantiklermcirony/ida-stateatlas).
- **Current scientific synthesis:** [The observer in the world](https://github.com/thantiklermcirony/boundedness-atlas/blob/main/PROGRAMME_DIRECTION.md).

The catalogue contains **41 manuscript records**, including historical versions. Publication, a mathematical proof, a passing software check, an empirical result and scientific priority answer different questions. The programme does not yet establish a complete theory of nature or a general predictive advantage across biology.

## Reproduce, challenge or contribute

Start with one result, its assumptions and its runnable source. A reproduction, stronger comparison, counterexample or independently calibrated dataset can move the work forward. [Contribution guide](https://github.com/thantiklermcirony/empirical-observatory/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md).

Engineering contributions also have inspectable records: [Ray Serve](https://github.com/ray-project/ray/pull/66039), [NeuroGym](https://github.com/neurogym/neurogym/pull/295), [Pertpy](https://github.com/scverse/pertpy/pull/1098), [Graphiti history](https://github.com/getzep/graphiti/pull/1867), and [Graphiti timestamps](https://github.com/getzep/graphiti/pull/1866). Follow each pull request for its review status; submission alone is not acceptance or validation of the wider framework.
